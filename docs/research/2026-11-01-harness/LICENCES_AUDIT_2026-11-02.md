Plus: model name prominently displayed on products with more than 100,000,000 MAU or US$20,000,000 monthly revenue (not us).

- Redistribution of the tensors is permitted, **provided the licence text travels with the copy**. Not OSI-approved; prize eligibility unanswered by hosts (not a licensing question for release).

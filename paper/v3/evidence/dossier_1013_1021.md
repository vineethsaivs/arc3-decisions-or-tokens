## 3. LoRA r16 rejection-sampling fine-tune

### What was done

1. **Data (10-16 to 10-17).** Arm `l1a1y8sv2r3hm32rl` (the pin's scored path plus the hm32 draft map, a request logger and a RE-ARC-3 loader; dev only) played 127 clean RE-ARC-3 games (no public family) on the GCP G4 VM at the replica's 4,032 slot-seconds per game, sampling T 0.7, top-p 0.95, top-k 20. Three Spot attempts (02: all 127, preempted; 03: 60 unfinished; 04: 48, complete), 18:10Z 10-16 to 07:43Z 10-17.
2. **Windows.** `build_rft_windows.py --reqlog` cut each request chain to the system prompt plus the latest suffix that fits 15,000 estimated tokens. A reply is a target (weight 1) when its level is one the run cleared; weight 2.0 when that (game, level) was cleared in some attempts and not others. Every turn of a cleared level counts: exploration, deaths, RESETs, the clearing turn.
3. **Training (10-18 to 10-19).** 1,400 random train windows (seed 0), LoRA r16 alpha 32 (merge scale 2.0) on GDN projections, attention q/k/v/o and the shared expert (300 tensors), routed int4 experts frozen; weighted token NLL at T 1 over the whole reply; AdamW lr 1e-4 cosine to 1e-5, accum 4, 350 steps, one pass. One crash (host OOM from a pinned-buffer leak, rc 137 at step 4) and a clean restart.
4. **T0 parity.** Trainer with adapter against the server with the adapter merged at load, 30 logged requests, under a gate revised on 10-18 before any training run.
5. **Replicas.** Arm `l1a1y8sv2r3hm32lora` (pin r3 + hm32 map + adapter folded into BF16 at load) ran two 25-game replicas at scale 2.0 (`~/runs/loraR1`, `loraR2`, 10-19) and one at scale 1.0 (alpha 16; `~/runs/loraH1`, 10-19 to 10-20, restarted twice after Spot preemptions), all on the GCP Compute Engine G4 VM, 14 streams, 7,200 s window. The control is the nine-run yield-8,192 Kaggle family (same as the v2 paper's D1 set).
6. **Decision.** Not promoted, not uploaded to Kaggle; the r16 line stopped on 10-20 by the half-strength rule.

## 5. trimtest: a paired decision test of leaner history

### What was done

When the harness trims its retained history, older reasoning and images are re-sent. A leaner rule ("leantrim": reasoning and images before the last 3 fresh turns become markers) would save about 3.5% of tokens if behaviour holds (`docs/research/2026-10-12-next/PLAN.md` 5.1, an inference). Instead of a replica, we asked the model directly: for each of 117 post-trim states from 24 of r3's 25 games (sb26 never trimmed), two replies with the logged history (full) and two with it slimmed (lean), on the pin's own server at live sampling (T 0.7, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, no seed, max 8,192 tokens). Full-versus-full disagreement is the noise floor. The gate was declared 2026-10-12 14:30Z before the pairs existed (`generated/gate-predeclared-2026-10-12-trimtest.json`); the run is Kaggle dev kernel `arc3-franzen-trimtest-a` v1 (2026-10-20, 2,597 s of kernel time, READY after 448 s, 468 replies, 0 errors). The same kernel also logged top-20 logprobs on 30 full-history replies (the K0 tail).

### What it shows

- **The training stack's forward pass is the noisiest instrument in round A.** Two passes of the same base on the same 66,949 tokens agree exactly on only about 12% of tokens. The RMS gap is 0.37 nats, and 17% of tokens leave the trust-region band. This holds the same within a process, across processes and across machines.
- **The raw k3 estimator is unusable here.** It spans 0.1 to 1,525 for the same comparison. A clipped k3 is stable (SD 0.0008), and so is a stop read net of the per-run floor.
- **The predeclared stops would have nulled WA on noise.** The defect was caught and amended before any trained-adapter reading.
- **Both finished members stopped early.** Each ended at the first val where entropy left the 10% band, kept a checkpoint that had seen 256 (WA) or 128 (WB) of 900 windows, and is non-null. At equal lr x steps their val readings nearly coincide.
- **Round A's supervision mostly pushes probability down.** In WA and WB it lowers the probability of tokens from failed plays. Entropy rises with dose while the probability of the pin's own held-out tokens falls. WC's public-only data, whose mass is balanced, has moved least so far.
- **The known-bad control moves outside the base's own band.** H moves reasoning length 17% below the base, so gate (a') can detect H's direction.

### What it does not show

- Nothing about levels or score. No wave, no probe and no (a') verdict exists for any member.
- That rising entropy or val NLL hurts play. The val set is held-out RE-ARC-3 pin play, and both readings measure distance from the pin.
- The cause of the forward nondeterminism, or whether it biases training. The band masks 11% to 12% of tokens at a zero adapter, so part of each step's mask is noise.
- That dose alone explains WB vs WA. WB at step 8 and WA at step 16 saw different numbers of windows.
- That WC differs from WA in kind. There is one checkpoint so far.

# v3 figures

Every figure is regenerated by one script in `paper/v3/analysis/figs/`, run from the repo root with
`uv run --no-project --with matplotlib --with numpy [--with scipy] python -I -B paper/v3/analysis/figs/<script>.py`.
Each script asserts the numbers it plots against its data files and the dossier, and fails if any label leaves the page or
overlaps another. Output: `<name>.pdf` (vector, 5.5 in wide) and `<name>.png` (200 dpi); Writeup gallery figures also get
`<name>_writeup.png` (1600 px wide). Shared style: `figstyle.py` (Okabe-Ito, gray baselines, one accent). Captions:
`CAPTIONS.md`.

Derived data written for these figures (`paper/v3/analysis/figs/data/`):
- `budget_band.json`: 200 run-level bootstrap resamples of the v2 budget curve (same simulator and regime as
  `paper/v2/analysis/f1_curves_n2.py` panel a), plus the full pool at the same 10 seeds.
- `yield_resume_by_game.csv`: resume openers per game, observed and replayed at 2,048 and 8,192, for the four runs with
  usage logs; built with `paper/v2/analysis/yield_mech.py`'s own functions and asserted to sum to `yield_mech.json`.
- `tried_arms.csv`: one row per measured arm (run directories of arm and control, lever class, mechanism fired or not,
  shipped or not, source record). Levels are recomputed from `yield_runs.json` and asserted.
- `board_draws_all_2026-10-31.csv`: 81 submissions, 2026-07-17 to 2026-10-31 (79 scored, 2 errored). `kapi.sh subs` returns only
  the newest 50, so the older rows come from the repo's stored submission snapshots (the union by submission id).
- `board_snapshots_2026-10-31.csv`: the last leaderboard snapshot of each UTC day with a rank (68 days from 2026-08-25).
- `clock_by_game_run.csv`, `gate_runs.csv`: per-run values behind F11 and F12 (seconds; levels and score).

## What could not be plotted faithfully, and what was decided

- **F6, RESETs and deaths:** no RE-ARC-3 training-play line. Training play ran 127 games (attempt 02) and 48 games
  (attempt 04) at the same per-game budget as a development run, so a whole-run count does not compare with a 25-game run; lora.md's per-turn rate
  (manual RESETs per 100 turns, 4.8 and 9.7) counts a different thing from our RESET actions per run and was read, not
  recomputed. The two lines that are drawn (reasoning characters per action, actions per reply) are lora.md's values,
  read and not recomputed (the dossier lists them as unverified). Three counters have 8 pin runs, not 9: the hm32 replica
  has no local transcripts.
- **F5 (b):** a per-game replay needs usage logs, which exist for four runs only (three at 2,048, one at 8,192). The
  other eight 8,192 runs are not in panel (b).
- **F4 band:** new for v3 (v2 had no curve band). It resamples the 8 runs with replacement and pools their attempts, at 10
  seeds per resample against 480 for the drawn curve. Resamples hold about 5.2 distinct runs, so the band runs below the
  full-pool curve near the plateau; the caption says so. The operating point is labelled "about 90% of its ceiling"
  (v2 claim C2: 94% of the drawn eight-run plateau, 85% to 91% of the ceiling extrapolated to more runs, shown as the
  hatched band). The constructed 2.5x world and v2's panel (b) are not redrawn
  (appendix material per craft_v3).
- **F3:** lever markers sit where each effect meets the SD 8.25 line (runs a test would need), not where each lever was
  tested. The +11% serving value (4.2 levels) is the budget curve's price, not a measured level change.
- **F7:** "mechanism fired" is my reading of each arm's gate record (rule: the counter the arm was built to move moved in
  the intended direction by what its gate asked). Not drawn: BF16 draft KV (0 levels, out of memory), arms with no level
  reading (NVFP4 KV probe, D-prime smoke run, keepdata and prompt fields riding inside other arms), leantrim (decided by
  the paired decision test, no replica) and GRPO round A (no level reading yet). Controls are the pooled byte-identical
  pair where one exists, so a few deltas differ from the ledger's control-at-the-time.
- **F1:** the three accent decisions are the coordinator's pick (2026-10-21): the 8,192-token yield, expert pruning that
  lets 14 games run at once, and the fail-closed checks. Fine-tuning is marked where it acts (the model box), in gray,
  because the adapter was not shipped.
- **F8:** Dodge's closed form uses the empirical distribution of the 7 draws, so it cannot exceed 40.98; a normal model
  is drawn beside it for that reason. A percentile band of the best-of-n was tried and dropped: on 7 discrete values it is
  a staircase that reads as noise.
- **Cover:** wording from FRAMING_v3 s9 (title "Which changes move the agent?"; arrows labelled only with questions or
  tested facts). Besides the title and the four labels it carries two axis words ("score", "tokens"); without them the
  arrows have no frame. The curve is a generic shape, not data; the noise band's half-width is drawn at SD 8.25 against
  the vertical arrows' +12.5 levels, so the lift is not drawn larger than the noise it sits in.
- **Review round 1 label changes (2026-10-21):** F2 (b) legend "serving variant (draft vocabulary)" and "40.98: best
  draw"; F6 "pin" became "no adapter" and the x axis "LoRA scale (0 = no adapter, 1 = as trained)"; F6 panel titles no
  longer state a trend; F1 labels define the opener and drop "banked 0.00"; F4 dot label says "extrapolated ceiling";
  F5 (b) legend names circles and triangles; F7's expert-pruning row now uses both startup-trim controls (92 and 97), so
  it reads +19.5 like Appendix A.
- **F12:** the scroll-report arm has n = 2 (third run not finished when drawn); the script reads whichever run directories
  exist and labels n. The pin arm mixes seven runs from two hosts (five Vertex, two Kaggle); the shaded band
  is the paper's 8.25-level SD of identical pairs, not the arm's own spread (7.76). No test is drawn: no arm differs from
  the pin by more than one SD of identical runs.
- **F11:** time stamps are wall clock at the start of a turn on shared serving, so a turn's clock includes queueing; the
  last turn of each game has no end stamp and is dropped. A game that cleared every level has no stuck tail by
  construction. "Idle" means the action counter did not move between two consecutive turns, which includes real analysis
  (simulators, history reads), not waste. Six runs, one game set; not a claim about the hidden games.
- **F10:** 50 early draws are collapsed into one gray family (everything scored before the Franzen fork draw of 2026-10-03,
  several harness lineages). Families after the fork follow the configuration names in the submission descriptions; the
  "later pins" family merges three configurations (keepalive variants and one other). Open markers sit at 0.00 (3 draws).
  Rank is not plotted over time: in September the field is packed at 3 to 8 points, so a fraction of a point moves our
  rank by hundreds (14, 293, 28 within two weeks at a best of 2.65 to 4.47); panel (b) shows our best score against the
  rank-1, 3 and 5 bar scores instead and prints the final rank (26 of 5,073 on the final public board, 2026-11-03 01:06 UTC).
  Rank 1 is missing before 2026-09-13 (not recorded).
- **F13 and F14 (new, harness modules under test):** `realmods.py` builds a temp copy of Franzen's patched tree with the overlays of
  arm l1a1y8sv2r3kp12lf15usc applied (the recipe of `solution/sim/test_franzen_seg8.py` and `test_franzen_scroll.py`) and loads the
  real `segmentation.py` and `frame_diff.py` before and after; nothing is reimplemented. Only bp35 and lf52 replay frames are
  copied (4.9 MB); the corpus recounts over all 22 replays are cached in `glyph_corpus.json` and `scroll_corpus.json` and rebuilt with
  `--rebuild <frames dir>` (replay dump: scratchpad `u3/frames`). F13 recount: the seg8 docstring's "71 of 184 starts in 13
  games" is not reproduced. Rule used: a `group` of 2 or more nodes with a bounding box of at least 3 x 3 cells (node bbox ends are
  inclusive): 76 of 184 starts in 13 games; also requiring fill (cells over bounding box area) of 0.1 or more, to drop sparse dot
  lattices: 73 in 13. No tried dot-texture rule gave 71; the docstring's rule is unstated. F14: the lf52 pair
  (frames 58 and 59) is on level 3, not level 1. The patched `changed_cell_count` is higher for bp35 (1,668 against 1,343)
  because the 24 rows that scrolled into view (1,536 cells) count as changed; the gain is in `moved` (89 to 11). For lf52 both fall
  (1,633 to 743 cells, 48 to 10 moved). Pairs counted: consecutive, same level, frame changed (6,313; fires 103 in bp35 of 363
  and 40 in lf52 of 772; silent 6,170, matching the test).

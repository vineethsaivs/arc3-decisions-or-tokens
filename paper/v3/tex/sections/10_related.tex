\section{Related work and credit}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Noise in evaluation.}
Single runs are weak evidence in reinforcement learning \citep{henderson2017matters} and for agents \citep{bjarnason2026randomness,anthropic2026infranoise}; agent benchmarks also lack holdouts and reproducibility standards \citep{kapoor2024agentsmatter}, and a paired effect of $+7.3$ points flipped to $-4.1$ on re-execution in one agent study \citep{alhamidi2026reexecution}.
Remedies include intervals \citep{agarwal2021precipice}, power analysis \citep{colas2018seeds,miller2024errorbars}, protocols judged by their error rates \citep{bouthillier2021variance} and the expected maximum of several runs \citep{dodge2019showyourwork}.
On this benchmark, forum posts attribute most of the variance of repeated runs to whether single levels clear \citep{borro19802026forum732932} and find that identical builds differ widely \citep{makarov2026forum743723}. A decomposition of an ARC-AGI-3 harness gap reports one counted run per configuration and game \citep{lencevicius2026persistgym}; on SWE-Bench the single-run pass@1 moves 2.2 to 6.0 points by the run chosen \citep{bjarnason2026randomness}. % N3-9
We add the size of that noise on this score, and concurrent replication on rented copies of the scoring GPU; we also tried a paired test of single decisions, used once and not validated.

\paragraph{A second population, and what it cannot test.}
As far as we found, public repeat-run data from other settings give similar sizes, and none tests our SD. Bjarnason et al.\ repeat 12 SWE-Bench-Verified agent cells ten times each \citep{bjarnason2026randomness}: pass@1 has SD 0.7 to 1.8 points (median 1.45) on means of 16\% to 64\%, a relative SD of 1.7\% to 8.1\% against our about 3\% to 7\%, so Eq.~\ref{eq:mde} puts one run per arm at about 6 points (5.7 at the median SD, 2.8 to 7.1 across cells). % V3-W53
Rebuilding a cross-section of Franzen-notebook copies from two daily board snapshots gives SD 3.0 to 3.7 points on 215 to 220 teams (Pham reports 25.8 with SD 3.9 and no stated count \citep{pham2026forum745062}); these are different teams, with edits, hardware and failed runs, and not repeats of one build. % V3-W54
Tufa Labs' public example run has 20 passes with SD 2.44 levels on a mean of 15.5 \citep{bessis2026duck,tufalabs2026duckcode}, and Franzen's notebook output four passes with SD 1.71 on 104.3 \citep{franzen2026milestone2}; both are within one job, so they contain no session-to-session shift and cannot test a shift like ours. % V3-W55
On ARC-AGI-3 itself, Lehmann and Vahdati repeat one frozen harness ten times over two months: SD 3.99 RHAE points on a mean of 48.5 (8\%), and the post hoc pick's original score sat 5.0 points above that mean \citep{lehmann2026harness}. Ari\~no de la Rubia and Pafka run six coding-agent pairings 52 times each: run SD (median 0.0107 AUC) exceeds the spread of the pairing means (0.0095), they size arms with the same $16\sigma^2/d^2$ rule as Eq.~\ref{eq:mde}, and on later data the gain kept about a third \citep{arino2026identical}. The scales and models differ from ours, so none of this confirms or refutes our 8.25 or the 5.6 around the shift; it shows that run SDs of several percent of the mean and a best-looking run above the mean are common. A fifteenth-place entrant likewise reports four scorings of one final notebook spread from 43.18 to 53.27 \citep{pomcho2026forum756379}, and the host says each game uses a fixed seed \citep{kamradt2026forum694153}, so the spread is not game randomness.

\paragraph{Budgets and context.}
Score that flattens with compute is the picture of \citet{pourcel2025soar}; budget forcing ends reasoning at a budget \citep{muennighoff2025s1}, while a yield resumes the turn.
Ending a request at a tool call forces recomputation \citep{abhyankar2024infercept}, and keeping reasoning across tool turns is a known harness rule and lever \citep{deepseek2025v32,kamradt2026astra}; we found no earlier measurement of a client-side yield threshold.
Tufa Labs note that hand-crafted tools hurt the Duck \citep{arcprize2026milestone1}, without numbers; our four shipped harness changes (leads $-1$ to $+6$ levels, three runs per arm) cannot exclude harm. % V3-W46

\paragraph{Self-training and reinforcement learning.}
Training on a model's own successes is the idea of STaR, ReST and rejection-sampling fine-tuning \citep{zelikman2022star,gulcehre2023rest,yuan2023rft}, and RL recipes already drop prompts that a policy always solves, as in DAPO's dynamic sampling \citep{yu2025dapo}.
Ours is the supervised case of the same problem: a success filter removes too little when the agent already succeeds, and what it keeps carries the agent's slow habits.
Round A follows GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath}, with its ratio band from the public RL code agent-factory-3 (near DAPO's asymmetric clip \citep{yu2025dapo}), its KL weight from GiGPO \citep{feng2025gigpo} and the $k_3$ estimator \citep{schulman2020klapprox}. % V3-G3
Serving outputs are known to depend on batch composition \citep{he2025nondeterminism,yuan2025numerical}; we measure what that does to a training-side drift guard.


\paragraph{ARC-AGI-3.}
Tycho and Twin use hosted models on the demonstration set \citep{lehmann2026tycho,skoutnev2026twin}, and the benchmark report warns that such scores do not measure progress \citep{arcprize2026arcagi3}.
The open-weight work we found is mostly write-ups \citep{bessis2026duck,franzen2026writeup}.
Public harnesses converge on an executable world model checked against the log of play \citep{skoutnev2026twin,lehmann2026tycho,wu2026kepler,gowtham2026arcskill,rodionov2026ablation}, the pattern of WorldCoder and PoE-World \citep{tang2024worldcoder,piriyakulkij2025poeworld}; they differ in whether the harness enforces the check or the prompt asks for it, and only Twin blocks an action until its program replays every observed transition. % V3-W33
Twin's ablation removes its whole harness at once, and Rodionov's verification variant ranks first in every setting but bundles verification with other changes, so neither isolates the replay; graph exploration reaches deep states at near-zero action efficiency \citep{rudakov2025graph}, and truncating history costs little when facts can be looked up again \citep{lencevicius2026persistgym}. % V3-W33
Our replay check is the optional, prompted form of that rule on an open-weight model: it fired, and three runs cannot say whether it helps (Sec.~\ref{sec:harness}, Appendix~\ref{app:research}). % V3-W29 V3-W33
The first-place team's forum write-up describes an RL-trained model used for the last three hours of its final run, and its author reports no isolated measurement of it \citep{chen2026forum755795}; the second-place team's method is not public. % V3-R1 V3-R2

\paragraph{What is new here.}
As far as we found, three things: a token-budget price for an ARC-AGI-3 agent, a two-setting measurement of a client-side yield threshold with an interleaved test on which its apparent gain did not reproduce, and a KL drift guard read net of a nondeterministic training forward pass; a paired test of single decisions is a fourth, but it was used once and is not validated.

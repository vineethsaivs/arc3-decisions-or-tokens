\section{Components: inherited, borrowed, ours}\label{app:components}

Table~\ref{tab:components} groups our 19 changes with their origin, and Table~\ref{tab:fullcomponents} lists every part of the configuration behind the 40.98 and 40.09 draws, called \texttt{l1a1y8sv2r3p} in our records (kernel version 1). Rows U1 to U7 are inherited parts that we did not re-measure; rows O1 to O19 are our changes, in build order, and their Origin cells say what each borrows. The notebook differs from the base fork in 5 of its 26 cells, through 29 asserted exact-string edits; 25 of them are active in the scored run. % A4 N-E6s0

For most changes the effect is a mechanism and not a level count: at two runs per arm a development comparison resolves shifts of about 23 levels (Sec.~\ref{sec:measure}). % A4 B4

\paragraph{Changes since 2026-10-13.} The pin moved to a draft-vocabulary variant (\texttt{l1a1y8sv2r3hm32p}, row O21) on 2026-10-14 after its gate passed, back to the configuration above for the 2026-10-20 draw, and on 2026-10-20 to that configuration plus the warmup keepalive (\texttt{l1a1y8sv2r3kp}, row O20). Neither change touches play. A fallback build that mounts our copies of the rival inputs (\texttt{l1a1y8sv2r3fbp}) passed its probe and stays unpinned. Later pins add 15 games in flight (2026-10-28) and the harness changes of Sec.~\ref{sec:harness}: the stall check and corner-joined regions (2026-10-31), the tool fixes (2026-11-01) and the replay check (2026-11-02, the final draw). % V3-H2 V3-K1 V3-K3 V3-B9 V3-W20 V3-W22 V3-W26 V3-W28 V3-W29

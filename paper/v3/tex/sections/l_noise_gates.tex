\section{Where the noise comes from and what the gates can see}\label{app:noisegates}

Three zero-GPU analyses of files already in the repository: \texttt{v3\_variance\_frames.py}, \texttt{v3\_gates\_board.py} and \texttt{v3\_second\_population.py} (Sec.~\ref{sec:related}), with outputs under \texttt{paper/v3/analysis/out/}.
All numbers are computed by those scripts; the claims table (Appendix~\ref{app:claims}) gives each source.

\paragraph{Per-game variance.}
A run sums 25 games. If the games vary independently, the variance of the run total is the sum of the per-game variances; a common run effect adds a positive covariance. Table~\ref{tab:pergame} compares the two.
In the three same-session sets of identical or near-identical runs the total sits at or below the sum (permutation $p$ 0.20, 0.48 and 0.21 for an observed total variance at or below independence), and independent games predict a run SD of 5.1, 6.3 and 5.5 against 3.9, 5.95 and 4.7 observed: no shared run effect is detectable inside a session. % V3-W50
The pairs are the exception. Their per-game variances sum to 38.8 (SD 6.2) but the total is 68.1 (SD 8.25), a covariance term of $+29.3$, which is the common shift of the pairs (Sec.~\ref{sec:measure}). The two interleaved arms have terms of $+15.0$ and $-40.5$ on six runs each, which we read as noise. % V3-W50 V3-W48
Five games hold 55\% to 64\% of the per-game variance in every frame. In the pool of 18 they are tn36 (18\% of the total), sc25 (12\%), su15 (9\%), sk48 (8\%) and s5i5 (7\%); four of 25 games never move (ar25 and cd82 clear every level every time; lp85 and sb26 never vary either), 21 move at all and 10 have a variance of 1 or more. % V3-W50

\paragraph{What the three-against-seven gate can see.}
The harness gate (Sec.~\ref{sec:harness}) ships a change if its three-run mean levels are at least the seven-run control's minus 2.5 and its mean score at least the control's minus 1.5, plus mechanism and action checks that we do not simulate. Table~\ref{tab:falseship} gives the probability of shipping a change of a given true size, with normal run noise and an independent control, at the three SDs of Sec.~\ref{sec:measure}; the score rule uses the 0.52 points per level that 18 harness runs fit (residual SD 1.7). % V3-W51
At SD 5.95 the gate ships a true loss of 6 levels one time in five (0.20, or 0.16 with the score rule), a null change seven times in ten (0.73, 0.67) and misses a true gain of 6 two times in a hundred (0.04 with the score rule). It cannot tell $+3$ from 0 (0.91 against 0.73). Two runs against seven, or three against three, behave the same (0.70 to 0.73 for a null change at SD 5.95).
The gate therefore screens out large losers and promotes nothing: a chain of changes that each passed it has no demonstrated lead, which is how we read the four shipped harness changes. % V3-W51 V3-W41

\paragraph{How much of 40.98 is selection.}
Sec.~\ref{sec:board} counts 17 valid draws of the agent's family from 2026-10-13 to 2026-11-02 (mean 34.59, SD 3.39; the adapter draws, the 10-12 draw of the earlier build and the 0.00 of 10-18 are not among them). The 21 post-yield nonzero draws are those 17 plus the three adapter draws (35.90, 39.79, 35.98, pinned by our override before their gate read; Sec.~\ref{sec:rl}) and the 10-12 draw (34.34), mean 34.96. Both sets are right; they are different sets. % V3-W52 V3-W17
Table~\ref{tab:maxdraws} gives the expected best of $n$ draws under three models and the chance that the best reaches 40.98. Under the family SD the expected best of 17 to 21 draws is 40.7 to 41.1; under the within-build SD, 39.6 to 40.2; the bootstrap gives 40.4 to 40.6. One family draw reaches 40.98 with probability 0.030 ($z$ 1.88), so 40.98 is a typical best of this many draws and not evidence that r3p beats the family: the build effect is not established (ANOVA across the seven builds $F$ 2.34, $p$ 0.113; between-build SD 2.1). % V3-W52
The two auto-selected draws are both r3p's. With r3p's mean of 38.43 over three draws and a reliability of 0.63, its shrunk mean is 37.0, 2.4 above the family mean. We therefore expect a private score of about 35 to 37 (the family mean to the shrunk r3p mean, per-draw SD about 3.4), not 40.98. The public and private halves hold different games, so the true mean can differ between halves by a term these data cannot estimate; the range is a prediction, not a result. % V3-W52
